Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 6, 2026  ·  Applies from: app version 1.1

Version 1.0 of the app collected no data at all. The previous version of this policy is in the repository's git history.

Meeple Clock ("the app") is a board game turn timer developed by Oz Soffy. This policy explains what information the app handles. The short version: everything you type into the app stays on your device. The app also reports anonymous usage statistics, which contain none of it.

What stays on your device

Everything you actually enter or create stays on your phone, in Apple's standard on-device storage:

  • Player names and colours you enter when setting up a game
  • Game history (game name, date, rounds played, and each player's timed totals)
  • Your app settings (such as default turn limit, sound, and haptics preferences)

None of this is ever transmitted off your device, and none of it is included in the usage statistics described below. It leaves your phone only if you choose to share it — for example by using the share button on a results screen, which hands the summary to the standard iOS share sheet under your control.

Deleting the app deletes all of this. You can also clear your game history at any time from the app's Settings screen.

Anonymous usage statistics

Starting with version 1.1, the app uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how it is used, so that development effort goes where it is actually useful.

What is collected

  • Which features are used and how — for example that a game was started in Countdown rather than Stopwatch mode, how many players it had, how long it ran, and how many rounds were played
  • Which screens are viewed
  • Basic device and environment information — device model, operating system version, app version, language, and approximate region (derived from network location, at country/region level)
  • A Firebase app instance ID: a random identifier generated on your device to distinguish one installation from another. It is not your name, email, phone number, Apple ID, or advertising identifier.

What is NOT collected

  • No player names. Nothing you type into the app is ever sent — not player names, not game names, not your saved history.
  • No accounts. You never sign up, sign in, or provide an email address.
  • No advertising identifier (IDFA), and no ads of any kind.
  • No contacts, photos, precise location, or health data.
  • No ability to identify you personally.

How it is used

To see which features get used and to find crashes and rough edges. Nothing more.

What it is never used for

The data is not sold, not shared with advertisers, and not used to track you across other companies' apps or websites. Because there is no cross-app tracking, the app does not need to — and does not — show an App Tracking Transparency prompt.

Analytics data is processed by Google as part of the Firebase service. Google's privacy policy is at policies.google.com/privacy, and Firebase's data handling is described at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Turning it off

The app has no in-app switch for this. If you would prefer not to send usage statistics, deleting the app stops all collection immediately.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children, does not ask for anyone's age, and collects no personal information from anyone, children included. The usage statistics above are anonymous and contain nothing a child (or anyone else) types into the app.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes these practices, this policy will be updated here before that version is released, and the change will be noted in the app's App Store release notes.
